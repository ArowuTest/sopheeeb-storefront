# Tree$hya’s Edit — Creator-led Design Direction

## Decision

The storefront is no longer a generic editorial boutique. It is a **high-energy extension of @tr33shya’s real content**: fitness equipment demos, shapewear try-ons, wellness finds, and fashion moments.

## Visual source of truth

Use the creator’s authorised public TikTok content in the initial experience:

- Tree$hya’s profile portrait anchors the hero and creator identity.
- Current oEmbed thumbnails from her own posts supply the hero collage, “In the video” cards, and category imagery.
- Every thumbnail links back to its corresponding TikTok post. No thumbnail is presented as a product listing or purchase destination.
- Once a product is approved in Creator Studio, it appears in the distinct **Live Edit** section with a real TikTok Shop CTA.

## Creative system

- **Tone:** direct, confident, warm, energetic, and personal—not quiet luxury or abstract art direction.
- **Palette:** near-black base, cream paper, electric tomato red, highlighter lime, pink accents, and warm creator photography.
- **Type:** Playfair Display for expressive headlines, DM Sans for body copy, DM Mono for TikTok-adjacent utility labels and video metadata.
- **Hero:** content collage with Tree$hya’s portrait, fashion video image, at-home Pilates content, profile proof, and an explicit TikTok watch CTA.
- **Content hierarchy:** identity → current TikTok content → lifestyle/fitness/fashion collections → approved shoppable products.
- **Interaction:** cards open the actual post; product cards retain the safe first-party TikTok Shop hand-off.

## Non-negotiables

1. No abstract placeholder art on the public home page.
2. No invented product imagery or fake purchasing actions.
3. Every video-derived image has a truthful source link to @tr33shya’s post.
4. The Creator Studio remains the only place to publish actual shop links.
5. The experience works as an energetic creator hub now and becomes a real storefront as products are imported and approved.
